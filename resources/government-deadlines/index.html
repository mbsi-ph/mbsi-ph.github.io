    MBSI Website Privacy Notice

    Last updated: August 27, 2026.

    Millennium Business Services, Inc. respects your privacy and processes personal data in accordance with Republic Act No. 10173, or the Data Privacy Act of 2012, its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and applicable issuances of the National Privacy Commission.

    This Notice explains how we process personal data collected through the MBSI website.

    Information We Collect

    Depending on how you use our website, we may collect:

    • Service and general inquiries: name, company, email address, telephone number, service of interest, and information contained in your message.
    • Job applications: name, contact details, employment and educational information, position or area of interest, application message, résumé or curriculum vitae, and other information you voluntarily provide.
    • Website updates: email address and subscription or consent records.
    • Website security and administration: limited technical information such as IP address, browser or device information, access logs, and form activity where necessary for website operation and security.

    We generally obtain this information directly from you when you complete a form, submit an application, subscribe to updates, or communicate with us through the website. Certain technical information may be collected automatically through our website and security systems.

    How and Why We Use Personal Data

    MBSI may process personal data to:

    • respond to inquiries and requests regarding our services;
    • communicate with prospective clients and business contacts;
    • evaluate and administer employment applications;
    • provide updates you have requested;
    • operate, maintain, and secure our website;
    • maintain appropriate business and recruitment records;
    • comply with legal and regulatory requirements; and
    • establish, exercise, or defend legal claims where necessary.

    Processing may be based on your consent, steps requested by you before entering into a contract, contractual necessity, compliance with legal obligations, or other lawful grounds permitted under the Data Privacy Act.

    Where sensitive personal information is involved, MBSI will process such information only where permitted by applicable law.

    MBSI does not use personal data collected through this website for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects on an individual.

    Who May Receive Your Personal Data

    Personal data may be accessed by authorized MBSI personnel who require it for legitimate business, recruitment, administrative, security, or compliance purposes.

    Where necessary, MBSI may engage service providers supporting website hosting, information technology, email, data storage, security, or recruitment. Personal data may also be disclosed to government authorities, regulators, courts, professional advisers, or other parties where required or permitted by law.

    MBSI does not sell personal data collected through this website.

    Retention and Security

    MBSI retains personal data only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected and for such additional period as may be required or permitted by applicable law, contractual obligations, legitimate business requirements, or the establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims.

    When personal data is no longer required for a lawful or legitimate purpose, it will be securely deleted, destroyed, or otherwise disposed of in accordance with MBSI’s retention and disposal procedures.

    MBSI implements reasonable and appropriate organizational, physical, and technical safeguards to protect personal data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, misuse, or unlawful processing.

    MBSI's Role in Providing Client Services

    This Notice applies primarily to personal data collected through the MBSI website.

    In providing outsourced business and professional support services, MBSI may process personal data on behalf of its clients. Depending on the processing activity, MBSI may act as a Personal Information Controller or as a Personal Information Processor acting under a client’s instructions.

    Such processing is governed by applicable laws, client agreements, data-processing arrangements, and MBSI’s internal privacy and information-security policies.
