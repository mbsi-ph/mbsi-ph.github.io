FAQs

Find answers to common questions about MBSI payroll, accounting, finance, compliance, and business process outsourcing services.

General Questions

Learn about working with MBSI, choosing services, and getting started.

Why should a company outsource accounting, payroll, finance, tax, and Xero services?

Outsourcing gives businesses access to experienced finance professionals without having to build and maintain a complete in-house team. It can reduce administrative workload, improve process consistency, and allow management to focus on core business activities.

Accounting support keeps bookkeeping, reconciliations, accounts payable and receivable, fixed asset records, and schedules organized and current. Payroll support helps process salaries, deductions, government requirements, final pay, and employee records accurately and confidentially. Financial reporting support improves reporting, analysis, cash-flow visibility, and preparation for external audits. Tax compliance support helps organize tax records, prepare and file returns, coordinate compliance requirements, and assist with tax assessments, planning, and payroll tax computation. Xero services assist with implementation, configuration, training, subscriptions, maintenance, and approved add-ons.

The client continues to make key business decisions while MBSI performs the agreed processes, reporting requirements, and support activities.

Can we outsource only one service?

Yes. A company may engage MBSI for a specific function, such as bookkeeping, payroll processing, financial reporting, or Xero support, without outsourcing all of its finance operations. Additional services may be introduced when the client’s requirements change.

Can MBSI work with our existing accounting, finance, or HR team?

Yes. MBSI can supplement an existing internal team by handling selected recurring processes, schedules, reconciliations, payroll activities, reporting requirements, or system-related tasks. Responsibilities, approvals, and communication arrangements are defined at the beginning of the engagement.

How does MBSI begin a new client engagement?

MBSI first discusses the client’s requirements, current processes, reporting expectations, systems, schedules, and areas requiring support. The service scope, responsibilities, required information, timelines, approval points, and communication arrangements are then defined before recurring work begins.

Can MBSI services scale with our business?

Yes. The scope of support may be adjusted as transaction volumes, staffing levels, reporting requirements, systems, or business operations change. Any material change in responsibilities or deliverables is reviewed and agreed upon by MBSI and the client.

How does MBSI protect business and employee information?

MBSI applies confidentiality procedures, controlled access, defined work processes, and secure handling practices for financial, payroll, employee, and business information. Specific access, transfer, storage, and reporting arrangements are established according to the nature of the engagement and the client’s requirements.

How can we request an MBSI service proposal?

Use the website’s Contact Us form to provide your organization name, contact information, service of interest, and a short description of your requirements. The MBSI team will review the inquiry and coordinate an initial discussion before preparing an appropriate service scope.

Accounting & Bookkeeping

Explore our bookkeeping, reconciliation, and accounting support.

What accounting and bookkeeping services does MBSI provide?

MBSI supports general accounting, transaction recording, bank and account reconciliations, accounting schedules, accounts receivable, accounts payable, fixed asset records, and recurring management reporting requirements. The exact activities and deliverables are defined with each client.

Can MBSI manage accounts payable and accounts receivable processes?

Yes. Depending on the agreed scope, MBSI can support customer invoicing, statements, collections coordination, supplier invoice processing, payment preparation, account reconciliation, and related schedules. Client approval controls remain part of the agreed process.

Does MBSI assist with reconciliations, fixed assets, and monthly schedules?

Yes. MBSI can prepare bank and balance-sheet reconciliations, maintain fixed asset records and depreciation schedules, and organize monthly accounting schedules and summaries. The frequency and format are aligned with the client’s reporting calendar.

Payroll Management

Find answers about payroll processing, employee records, and recurring payroll tasks.

What activities are included in MBSI’s payroll management services?

Payroll support may include gross-to-net salary computation, deductions, government contributions, payroll reporting, required forms, employee payroll records, and other agreed recurring payroll activities. The final scope is based on the client’s workforce and payroll requirements.

Does MBSI assist with government deductions and required payroll forms?

Yes. MBSI can support the computation and recording of applicable employee deductions and government contributions, together with the preparation of agreed payroll-related forms and schedules. Client-specific filing and approval responsibilities are confirmed during the engagement.

Can MBSI process final pay and protect confidential payroll information?

Yes. MBSI can support final-pay computations and related payroll adjustments when these activities are included in the service scope. Payroll and employee information is handled through defined processes, controlled access, and confidentiality procedures.

Financial Reporting

Understand our financial reporting, analysis, and audit preparation support.

What financial reports can MBSI prepare?

MBSI can prepare recurring financial reports, management summaries, supporting schedules, account analysis, cash-flow information, and other agreed reports. The format, frequency, source information, and review process are defined with the client.

Does MBSI assist clients during external audits?

Yes. MBSI can assist by preparing schedules, organizing supporting documents, coordinating information requests with the client’s independent external auditor, and helping management respond to audit requirements.

MBSI’s role is to support the client during the audit. It does not replace the independent external auditor or issue an external audit opinion.

Does MBSI provide tax, corporate, and internal-control support?

Depending on the engagement, MBSI can support tax reporting and submission, tax-assessment preparation, corporate documentation, internal audit activities, and control reviews. The scope is defined according to the client’s requirements and does not replace advice or assurance that must be provided by an independent licensed professional.

Tax Compliance

Learn about tax reporting, assessment assistance, planning, and compliance coordination.

What tax compliance support does MBSI provide?

MBSI supports tax reporting and submission, tax assessment assistance, tax planning, and payroll tax computation. The agreed service scope determines the tasks and supporting records involved.

Can MBSI help prepare tax returns and supporting documents?

MBSI provides preparation and filing support for recurring tax returns, related schedules, and supporting documentation, including coordination of income tax, VAT, percentage tax, fringe benefit tax, withholding tax, and documentary stamp tax requirements within the agreed scope.

Does MBSI assist with tax assessments and planning?

MBSI assists with supporting documents and review during tax examinations and assessments. Tax planning support includes reviewing procedures and practices that may create tax exposure and identifying areas for process improvement.

How can we discuss our tax compliance requirements?

Use the Contact Us form to describe your business and the tax compliance assistance you need. Our team will coordinate a discussion of your requirements and the appropriate service scope.

Xero Services

Explore Xero implementation, training, administration, and ongoing support.

What Xero services does MBSI provide?

MBSI provides practical support for Xero system implementation, configuration, user training, administration and maintenance, software subscriptions, and approved customizations or add-ons. The service scope depends on the client’s accounting processes and system requirements.

Can MBSI implement Xero and train our users?

Yes. MBSI can help configure Xero around the client’s approved chart of accounts, users, workflows, and reporting needs. Practical end-user training can also be provided to help designated staff use the system consistently.

Does MBSI support Xero subscriptions, maintenance, and add-ons?

Yes. MBSI can assist with software subscriptions, user administration, ongoing maintenance, workflow reviews, and suitable approved third-party add-ons. Recommendations are based on the client’s accounting and reporting requirements.
